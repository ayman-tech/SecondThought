Free Chrome extension

A second thought, right in your browser.

Make a draft more professional or soften harsh language right where you write. It works offline after setup, and your drafts stay on your device.

  • Catch rough wording as you type. Get a nudge for harsh or casual language, run an optional tone check, or turn nudges off for a site.
  • Change only what needs work. Rewrite flagged lines or selected text, see exactly what changed, then copy, replace, or undo.
  • Keep key details intact. The extension keeps the original wording if a rewrite changes a recognized number, date, or amount.
