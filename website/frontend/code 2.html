Activation Steering

The Steering Lab's runtime residual intervention engine.

  • arrow_forward Zero Weight Changes: Base weights remain pristine. Interventions occur in the hidden residual stream during forward pass.
  • arrow_forward Continuous Parameter Dialing: Slide $\alpha$ smoothly to shift tone incrementally from informal to hyper-academic.
  • arrow_forward Multi-Objective Superposition: Combine orthogonal vectors simultaneously: $\mathbf{h} + \alpha_1 \mathbf{v}_{\text{formal}} + \alpha_2 \mathbf{v}_{\text{empathy}}$.
  • info The Honest Reality: Non-linearities can cause coherence degradation at extreme $\alpha$ values—making empirical lab exploration essential.

group ABOUT SECOND THOUGHT

An Open Lab for Controllable Language Mechanics

Second Thought was founded at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability research and everyday communication tools. We believe that understanding how language models construct tone is prerequisite to making them safe, respectful, and genuinely controllable.

biotech

Interpretability First

Rather than treating LLMs as mysterious black boxes steered purely by prompt engineering, we isolate residual control vectors to understand the geometric structure of language attributes.

lock_open

Open Research Stance

We publish our failure modes alongside our breakthroughs. Activation steering is fragile, polysemantic, and still imperfect—we invite the broader NLP community to test our findings.

laptop_chromebook

Practical Everyday Utility

Our research feeds directly into practical tools. The Second Thought browser extension delivers on-device, respectful rewriting for emails and code reviews without sending text to any cloud server.

"We do not claim activation steering is solved. Residual spaces are dense and non-linear. But by allowing users to touch the internal parameters directly, we transform abstract math into an intuitive creative experience."

— The Second Thought Research Group
