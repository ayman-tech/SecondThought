A quick guide

Colours show held-out pair ordering, not measured rewrite quality.

Single layer: clicking a layer replaces the enabled layer.

Focused vector: negative_layer11_mean · raw

All listed nonzero shifts are applied together, at every token. Combining layers can cause repetition or change meaning.

  1. Pick a layer. Single layer mode replaces the enabled layer when you click a panel. Multi-layer mode lets you inspect panels and check Enable layer to add each one.
  2. Choose a vector family. Pick Formality or Detoxification and mean or last-token pooling. Select layers and adjust their strengths to apply this family.
  3. Try a sentence. Your rewrite updates automatically; highlights show changed words.

Preview layers have no saved vector and won’t affect the rewrite. The moving lines are illustrative.

Set all strengths to zero or deselect all layers to compare without steering.

Each feature keeps its own layers, mode, pooling and strengths. Choose Edit layers for to change the feature shown in the layer diagram. The enabled-layer chips show each retained strength. Click × to disable a layer, or Clear layers for a prompt-only baseline. Returning to Single layer keeps the focused enabled layer, or the first enabled layer. Reset restores Single layer mode.

Layer colours: darker teal means higher held-out pair ordering for the feature and pooling you chose. The number is the percentage of held-out pairs whose target-minus-source activations project positively onto that exact direction. A 50% reference is shown; colours are not adjusted against shuffled controls.

Compare within the same feature, pooling and extraction cohort. Hover or focus a layer for its score and Wilson 95% interval. Overlapping intervals do not establish a reliable ranking. Grey means no recorded measurement for that exact vector, not ineffective steering.

Colours describe the selected feature and pooling vectors. Reset selects Formality, mean pooling, layer 11 at +0.5. Fluency uses human-annotated document revisions; its effects on other tasks remain experimental. Pair ordering measures activation separation, not response quality or meaning preservation.
