EVALUATION

How we measure a rewrite.

Toxicity, formality, meaning and facts, measured offline on a fixed set of workplace messages. These are diagnostic signals, not a guarantee that any single rewrite is correct.

HOW A REWRITE IS CHECKED

One message, two passes, a fact check after each.

  1. 1SpottingWord lists catch swearing, insults, texting shorthand and shouting, on your device. If none match, a tone check can score each line when you ask. Only flagged lines go on.
  2. 2Detoxify passThe model removes insults and hostility, sentence by sentence.Toxicity
  3. 3Facts guardNumbers, dates, amounts, links and IDs must survive unchanged.Facts kept
  4. 4Formalize passThe same model makes the wording professional.Formality
  5. 5Facts guardThe check runs again on the final wording.Facts kept
  6. 6SuggestionYou see the rewrite and decide whether to use it.Meaning kept
Toxicity
0 to 1 from the Detoxify classifier. Lower is better.
Formality
0 to 1 from a RoBERTa formality ranker, scored per sentence. Higher is more formal.
Meaning kept
Sentence-embedding similarity between original and rewrite. 1 means the same meaning.
Facts kept
Share of messages where every number, date, amount, link and ID survives exactly.
